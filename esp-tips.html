ESP Tips & Tricks

Advice for each stage of the Employer Set Project (ESP).

Understanding the Brief

  • Read the brief at least twice and highlight what the employer actually wants.
  • Write the problem in one sentence in your own words.
  • Make a list of any questions and ask them early.

Research

  • Look at how other companies have solved similar problems.
  • Keep a list of every source you use as you go, not at the end.
  • Think about who the end users are and what they need.

Planning

  • Split the work into small tasks and give each one a deadline.
  • Use a simple plan such as a Gantt chart or a to-do board.
  • Leave extra time for testing and fixing problems.

Design and Development

  • Sketch wireframes or flowcharts before you start coding.
  • Save your work often and keep older versions (for example with Git).
  • Test each part as you build it, and write down what you tested.

Presenting to the Employer

  • Explain how your solution meets each point in the brief.
  • Practise your presentation and keep slides short.
  • Be ready to answer questions about why you made your choices.

Reflection

  • Be honest about what went well and what did not.
  • Say what you would do differently next time.
  • Name the skills you developed, both technical and teamwork.
