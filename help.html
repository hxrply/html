Help

Answers to common questions about this site.

How do I get back to the home page?

Click Home in the menu, or "Chelmsford College" in the top-left corner of any page.

Where do the links go?

  • Home takes you back to the home page.
  • Chelmsford College opens the college website in a new tab.
  • T Levels opens the official T Levels website in a new tab.
  • ESP Tips & Tricks has advice for each stage of the Employer Set Project.
  • Help is this page.

Who can I ask for more help?

Speak to your course tutor, or contact the college through the Chelmsford College website.
