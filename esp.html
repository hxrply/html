My Employer Set Project

Replace the text in each section below with your own work. Copy a section to add more.

The Brief

What has the employer asked you to do? Who is the employer and what problem are they trying to solve?

Research

What did you find out? List your sources and the key points you learned.

Planning

How did you plan the project? Include timelines, tasks and who did what.

My Solution

Describe what you made or proposed and why it meets the brief.

Presentation

How did you present your work to the employer? What feedback did you get?

Reflection

What went well, what would you do differently, and what skills did you develop?
